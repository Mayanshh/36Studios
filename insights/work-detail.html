The Objective

Translating absolute physical perfection into an ethereal digital environment.

Aethelgard builds flawless structural marvels. Yet, their online architecture resembled standard corporate platforms. They came to 36 Studios demanding an online existence that commanded the exact same awe and expensive presence as stepping inside their physical spaces.

The Strategy

Choreographing layout variables around human visual weight mechanics.

We abandoned generic templates and designed a heavy, intentional scrolling environment leveraging lightweight custom WebGL shaders. Every layout variable responds gracefully to desktop interactions, generating micro-delights that guide highly discerning clients precisely into their exclusive curation funnels.

The Execution

Anatomy of the Curation

01 / Cinematic Motion

GSAP Orchestration

Every image reveal uses dynamic layout calculations. By offloading complex animations directly to GPU render layers, transitions remain crystal clear at a fixed 120 frames per second on high-end displays.

02 / Edge Distribution

Zero Friction Deployment

Instead of complex server management frameworks, the final build was compiled using standard edge nodes on AWS. This architecture maps asset delivery speeds under 1.4s globally, completely eliminating standard performance friction.
